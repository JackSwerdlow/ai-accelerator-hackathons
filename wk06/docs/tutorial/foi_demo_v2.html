Dear Requester, Freedom of Information Act 2000 – Response to Your Request Thank you for your request received by this department. We have considered your request carefully in accordance with our obligations under the Freedom of Information Act 2000 (FOIA 2000). Please note that the statutory deadline for responding to FOI requests is 20 working days from the date of receipt. Your request, as submitted, seeks access to an extraordinarily broad range of departmental records without a defined subject matter, specific timeframe, or meaningful scope. It encompasses, among other things, all departmental documents and emails, HR and personnel records, financial records, legal advice, security incident reports, and fraud reports spanning an extended historical period. For the reasons set out below, we are withholding the information requested in full. ——— 1. SECTION 12 – COST LIMIT (ABSOLUTE EXEMPTION) Under section 12 FOIA 2000, a public authority is not obliged to comply with a request where the cost of doing so would exceed the appropriate limit prescribed by the Secretary of State (currently £600 for central government, equivalent to 24 hours of staff time at the prescribed rate). Your request, as framed, encompasses virtually all information held by this department across all functions and over an extended period. Locating, retrieving, reviewing, and redacting the volume of information falling within scope would self-evidently and substantially exceed this cost limit. Section 12 is an absolute exemption and no public interest test is required. We would encourage you to consider refining and resubmitting your request with a narrower and more specific subject matter, defined timeframe, and clearly identified categories of information. We would be happy to assist you in scoping a more focused request. ——— 2. SECTION 40 – PERSONAL DATA (ABSOLUTE EXEMPTION) To the extent that any information falling within the scope of your request constitutes personal data of identifiable individuals — including information held within HR, pay, disciplinary, grievance, recruitment, performance, and expenses records — such information is exempt from disclosure under section 40(2) FOIA 2000. Disclosure of such personal data would be incompatible with the data protection principles under the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. Section 40 is an absolute exemption and no public interest test applies. No personal details of any individual are disclosed in this response. ——— 3. SECTION 42 – LEGAL PROFESSIONAL PRIVILEGE (QUALIFIED EXEMPTION) Your request encompasses all legal advice obtained by the department. Such information is exempt under section 42 FOIA 2000, which protects information subject to legal professional privilege. Public Interest Test: We have considered whether the public interest in disclosure outweighs the public interest in maintaining this exemption. The public interest in maintaining legal professional privilege is well established: disclosure would undermine the department's ability to obtain full and frank legal advice, which is essential to the proper conduct of public administration. Your request does not identify any specific public interest that would outweigh this consideration. Accordingly, the balance of the public interest favours withholding this information. ——— 4. SECTIONS 24 AND 31 – NATIONAL SECURITY AND LAW ENFORCEMENT (QUALIFIED EXEMPTIONS) Your request encompasses security incident reports and fraud reports held by the department. This information is exempt under section 24 (national security) and/or section 31 (law enforcement) FOIA 2000, as applicable. Public Interest Test: We acknowledge the general public interest in transparency regarding security and fraud matters. However, disclosure of security incident reports and fraud reports — whether relating to live or historical matters — could prejudice ongoing or future investigations, compromise security arrangements, and undermine law enforcement activity. The balance of the public interest favours withholding this information. ——— RIGHT OF APPEAL If you are dissatisfied with this response, you have the right to request an internal review. Please submit your request for an internal review in writing within 40 working days of the date of this letter, addressed to this department's FOI team. If, following an internal review, you remain dissatisfied, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), which can be contacted at: Information Commissioner's Office Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire, SK9 5AF Website: www.ico.org.uk Yours faithfully, FOI Response Officer [Department Name] [Date]
