## Human-in-the-Loop Requirements (Principle 4)

> "You have meaningful human control at the right stages."

- **High-risk decisions must involve human validation.** Humans must validate any high-risk decisions influenced by AI.
- **Fully automated decision-making should be avoided** for "significant decisions, such as those involving someone's health or safety." FOI release/withhold decisions affecting individuals fall into this category.
- **User feedback mechanisms are mandatory.** Systems must allow users to report issues and prompt a human review, since AI can produce "unwanted or inaccurate results."
- Real-time human review is not always feasible, but oversight must occur "at other stages of the AI's development and deployment."

## Transparency and Explainability

- **AI-generated communications must be identified as such.** Responses written by automated AI should "include something like 'this response has been written by an automated AI chatbot'."
- Government departments are **required to use the Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS)** to document and publicly disclose algorithmic tools used in decision-making.
- Some architectures make it "challenging to trace how a specific input leads to a specific output" — explainability must be considered at design time, not retrofitted.

## Audit Trail and Monitoring (Principle 5)

> "You understand how to manage the full AI life cycle."

- **Continuous monitoring during deployment:** robust assurance and regular checks of the live tool are required.
- **Drift detection:** systems must "monitor and mitigate for potential drift, bias, and hallucinations."
- **Performance measurement:** track whether "users' needs and business goals are being met," not just model accuracy metrics.
- Regular user research should build "continuous feedback loops."

## Governance Structures Required

An organisation deploying an AI FOI-processing tool would need:

| Governance element | Requirement |
|--------------------|-------------|
| AI governance board | Senior leaders and experts to set principles and review/authorise AI uses |
| Escalation process | Documented review and escalation processes for issues |
| Risk management | Connect assurance teams early in the project life cycle |
| ATRS publication | Mandatory for tools with significant influence on public-effect decisions |

## Citizen-Facing Obligations

- User research is foundational before deployment.
- Accessibility testing is mandatory.
- Citizens should be told openly "where and how algorithms and AI systems are being used in official duties."
